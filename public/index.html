Privacy Policy (GDPR, CCPA & Local-First)

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Core Architecture & Data Minimization

f0undr AI operates on strict data minimization principles. We do not require accounts, logins, email addresses, or phone numbers to use the platform.

2. Storage of Conversations & Memories

Your conversation histories, persistent memories, and customized settings are stored exclusively inside your browser's window.localStorage on your local client device. They are never synchronized to private f0undr databases or retained on central servers.

3. No Sale or Commercialization of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade user prompts, screen captures, or search queries with any third-party data brokers, ad networks, or external aggregators.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the absolute right to access, export, and delete your data at any time. You can use our built-in Export Chats tool or trigger the Purge Data button to execute an immediate, permanent client-side wipe.
